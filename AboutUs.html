History

Kenya Marines Supplies Ltd was founded in 2005 with the mission to provide high-quality marine products and services to the maritime industry in Kenya. Over the years, we have grown to become a trusted name in the industry, known for our commitment to excellence and customer satisfaction.

Mission

Welcome to Kenya Marines Supplies Ltd, your trusted partner in marine solutions!

Our mission is to provide top-notch marine products and services that ensure the safety, efficiency, and reliability of your maritime operations.

Vision

Our vision is to be the leading provider of marine solutions in Kenya, recognized for our innovation, quality, and customer-centric approach.
